Teachers, lecturers and schools can use an Oz Box to give AI agents the preparation and admin work (drafting materials,
writing questions, summarising, scheduling) while keeping every decision about students with people, and keeping student
data inside the school's own cloud account.

See [how to read the status](/use-cases#how-to-read-the-status). Everything here is **Preview** at most: built and tested, not
yet run end to end by a school on a real box.

**Ground rules for every example:**
- **The agent drafts; the teacher decides.** It never grades, sanctions or decides anything about a student.
- **Student data stays in your account.** Keep it on the box, use a local model where your rules require it, and follow your
  local privacy rules (for example FERPA in the United States, or GDPR-style rules elsewhere).
- **Children's safety first.** Agents work for staff. Do not give students unsupervised access to an agent that can reach the
  internet; use the sandboxed setups below.

## 1. Course materials from a syllabus

**You want:** lesson plans, slides and handouts drafted from your syllabus, for you to adapt.

Put the syllabus on the box and ask from chat: *"Draft week 3's lesson plan, slides outline and a one-page handout."*

**Underneath:** a supervised run; drafts are saved in the course folder. A skill can hold your school's lesson-plan template.
**Limits:** check facts and level; you know your class.
**Status:** Preview.

## 2. Quizzes and assignments

**You want:** questions at chosen difficulty levels, with answer keys and marking notes.

**Underneath:** drafts follow your curriculum notes in memory; you review and edit before use.
**Limits:** always check answers; models make mistakes, especially in maths.
**Status:** Preview.

## 3. Feedback drafts on student work

**You want:** suggested comments on student work against your rubric, to save time while marking.

**Underneath:** the agent reads the work and the rubric and drafts comments; grades are entered by the teacher.
**Limits:** **the agent never grades.** Use it for comment drafts only, and follow your school's policy on AI in assessment.
**Status:** Preview.

## 4. Admin, timetables and reminders

**You want:** timetable clashes found, reminders to parents drafted, attendance summarised.

```
ozbox schedule add --name attendance --when "every friday at 3pm" --engine claude-code --box school \
  --prompt "Summarise this week's attendance from /work/attendance; list students below 90%" --results-to alert
```

**Underneath:** the summary comes to staff on the alert channel; messages to parents are drafts for a person to send.
**Limits:** attendance data is personal data; keep it on the box and limit who can use the agent.
**Status:** Preview.

## 5. A supervised research assistant for a class

**You want:** students to use an AI research assistant in a controlled way, with a record of what it did.

One box per class, with approved skills (for example "cite every source") and the audit log on, used through a teacher's
session.

**Underneath:** the permission engine limits what the agent may do (read-only by default); every action is in the audit log.
**Limits:** the agent works under staff accounts; do not hand students direct agent access without your school's approval.
**Status:** Preview.

## 6. Accessible versions of materials

**You want:** simplified versions, transcripts and captions of your materials for students who need them.

**Underneath:** drafts are saved beside the original; a local model can process recordings inside your account.
**Limits:** have specialists check materials for specific needs.
**Status:** Preview.

## 7. Language practice partner

**You want:** conversation practice with corrections, in a contained setup.

**Underneath:** a dedicated box with a local model and no access to the open internet beyond what you allow.
**Limits:** a supervised, staff-run setup; not a replacement for a teacher.
**Status:** Preview.

## 8. Teaching computing on disposable machines

**You want:** every student gets the same ready-made machine for a lab, thrown away afterwards.

```
ozbox create lab-template --profile python-class
ozbox snapshot lab-template
ozbox clone lab-template student-01      # one per student, from the same snapshot
ozbox destroy student-01                 # after class
```

**Underneath:** clones start from the same snapshot; nothing carries over between classes.
**Limits:** each running box is billed by the hour; pause or destroy them after class. Cloning works on AWS today.
**Status:** Works (AWS: create, snapshot, clone and destroy have run on a real box); the classroom routine itself is Preview.

## 9. School operations reports

**You want:** enrolment, attendance and budget summaries for leadership each month.

**Underneath:** a scheduled run reads the exports you provide and writes a short report with the source of every figure.
**Limits:** reports are drafts for the leadership team to check.
**Status:** Preview.

## 10. Private by design

**You want:** to use AI with student information without sending it to an outside service.

Run a local model on the school's box (Settings → Models → Set up Ollama on any box) and point the runs at it; keep the box in
the school's own cloud account and region.

**Underneath:** the model and the data stay in your account; the audit log records every access.
**Limits:** local models on CPU boxes are slower and less capable than large hosted models.
**Status:** Preview (local models have not been run on a real box yet).
